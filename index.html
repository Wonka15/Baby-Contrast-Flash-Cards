A NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS

Small cards. Shared moments.

Little Look is a simple visual-play companion. Its cards are designed to be explored with a caregiver, not as a replacement for interaction, reading, or play.

Sounds are generated in your browser and do not stream or record audio. The melody is an original, gentle synthesized tune.

Future-friendly by design: language labels and collections can be expanded to support Spanish.
